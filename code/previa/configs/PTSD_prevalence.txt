- Women are more likely to develop future PTSD
- Younger people are more at risk of developing future PTSD
- Higher trauma severity is associated with a higher future PTSD risk
- Experiencing sexual or physical assault makes it more likely to develop future PTSD
- People with a lower socioeconomic status are at greater risk of developing future PTSD
- Prior mental health issues increase the risk of developing future PTSD
- People who experienced another trauma before are at greater risk
- Lower education level is associated with higher future PTSD risk
- Peri-traumatic dissociation is associated with higher future PTSD risk
- Shame and anger with others is associated with higher future PTSD risk
- High levels of reexperiencing or arousal symptoms is associated with higher future PTSD risk
- Loss of interest and emotional numbness appeared to be the strongest predictors of  future PTSD
